PrimeFacts (2025)

The dataset includes metadata derived from publicly available PolitiFact articles and automatically extracted evidence (leaks, premises) produced as part of academic research.

All derived materials (metadata and extracted evidence) are released under the Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC 4.0).

Original PolitiFact content (including full article texts, author biographies, and speaker descriptions) remains the property of PolitiFact and The Poynter Institute. These materials are not redistributed as part of this dataset. Access to full texts is available upon request for non-commercial academic research purposes only.

1. Attribute both PolitiFact (as the original content source) and PrimeFacts (as the derived dataset),
2. Include a link to the CC BY-NC 4.0 license, and
3. Indicate if modifications were made.
